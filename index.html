Divine Recipes — For Eternal Knowledge

How it works

1Snap it

Photograph your refrigerator or your pantry. The app reads what’s there, and you confirm the list — you stay in charge of what it sees.

2See your matches

Get real recipes ranked by what you already have, with a clear count of what you’ve got and what you’d still need. Your leftovers are dated and tracked, so nothing good quietly goes to waste.

3Cook it

Cook the meal, save the money, and keep the food out of the trash. Ten lookups a month are free; premium is $2.99 a month.

Leftovers, handled safely

Refrigerate promptly. Use within 4 days. First in, first out. And when in doubt, throw it out.

The part nobody else does

Your recipes belong here too. Suggest a recipe of your own, and if it passes a professional chef’s check, it gets published in the app with your name on it.

Every member recipe is checked by a professional chef before it is published — the app’s chef is Jonathan Bellamy, French-trained, the founder of Fresh Jon Innovations. No recipe goes out with his name on the process until it has actually been checked.
